What the criteria say. The condition for Qualified requires the lead, in their own words, to do at least one of these: answer the agent's qualifying questions; provide the information asked for (name, course, qualification or marks, location, availability, current status); show interest or intent; ask a question about the offering; or agree to a callback. It then adds an explicit exclusion: "…do not yield this value from a bare affirmative to the opening alone ('yes, go ahead,' 'sure') with no further engagement." And Not Sure is defined for exactly the opposite case: "…yield this value only when engagement cannot be established either way — there was no meaningful conversation (call disconnected early, silence, only a greeting)." Every case below fails the first test and matches the second.
